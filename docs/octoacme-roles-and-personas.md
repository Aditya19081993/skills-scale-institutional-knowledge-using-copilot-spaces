# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Works with Product Managers on feature requirements
- Collaborates with QA/Testing on acceptance criteria
- Partners with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers on deployment
- Consults with Security Specialists on security requirements

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Incorporate feedback from Customer Support Liaisons

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Aligns with Project Managers on delivery timelines
- Collaborates with UI/UX Designers on user experience
- Works with Business Analysts to define requirements
- Partners with Customer Support Liaisons on user feedback

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Syncs weekly with Product Managers on priorities
- Facilitates communication between Developers and Business Analysts
- Escalates risks identified by Security Specialists and DevOps Engineers
- Coordinates cross-functional dependencies with all roles

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business requirements into actionable, technical deliverables. They bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring clarity and alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Create detailed requirement specifications and user stories
- Identify gaps, ambiguities, and dependencies early
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure technical feasibility
- Support QA/Testing by clarifying requirements and edge cases

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and traceable
- Reduce rework and misalignment between business and technical teams
- Accelerate understanding and reduce estimation uncertainty

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and acceptance criteria
- Clarification sessions with stakeholders and Developers
- Requirement review meetings during planning
- Ad-hoc support during execution for requirement clarifications

### Key Interactions
- Partners closely with Product Managers on requirement prioritization
- Collaborates with Developers during planning and estimation
- Works with UI/UX Designers to incorporate user experience needs
- Supports QA/Testing with requirement validation
- Escalates scope clarifications to Project Managers

---

## UI/UX Designers

### Role Summary
UI/UX Designers are responsible for creating intuitive, user-centered experiences. They conduct user research, create wireframes and prototypes, design visual interfaces, and validate usability.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and journey mapping
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Develop visual designs and design systems
- Perform usability testing and iterate based on feedback
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure design feasibility
- Create design documentation and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Minimize user friction and improve adoption
- Maintain visual and interaction consistency

### Typical Communication
- Design docs, wireframes, and prototypes
- Design review meetings with stakeholders
- Collaboration with Developers on implementation
- Usability testing reports and iteration recommendations

### Key Interactions
- Works closely with Product Managers on user needs and goals
- Collaborates with Developers on technical feasibility
- Partners with Business Analysts to understand workflows
- Supports QA/Testing with expected user interactions
- May coordinate with Customer Support Liaisons on user pain points

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers ensure smooth deployment, continuous integration, and system reliability. They manage infrastructure, automate deployment pipelines, and monitor production systems.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure (cloud, on-premise, hybrid)
- Automate deployment and rollback processes
- Monitor system performance, uptime, and logs
- Collaborate with Developers on deployment readiness
- Provide runbooks and incident response procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Maintain infrastructure security and compliance

### Typical Communication
- Deployment pipelines and automation scripts
- Release coordination and deployment planning
- Incident response and postmortem documentation
- Infrastructure and performance dashboards

### Key Interactions
- Works closely with Developers on deployment requirements
- Collaborates with Security Specialists on infrastructure security
- Partners with Project Managers on release coordination
- Provides guidance to QA/Testing on staging and production environments
- Participates in risk register reviews for deployment risks

---

## Security Specialists

### Role Summary
Security Specialists review projects for security risks, identify vulnerabilities, and guide teams in building secure systems. They ensure compliance and drive security best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review project security requirements and architecture
- Conduct security assessments and threat modeling
- Identify and triage security vulnerabilities
- Consult with Developers on secure coding practices
- Review code and infrastructure for security risks
- Maintain and update security policies and guidelines

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Build security into the development process
- Ensure compliance with security standards and regulations

### Typical Communication
- Security review reports and vulnerability findings
- Security best practice guidance and standards
- Threat models and risk assessments
- Incident response and security postmortems

### Key Interactions
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on security priorities
- Collaborates with Developers on secure implementation
- Partners with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers on infrastructure security
- Reviews work with QA/Testing on security test coverage
- Escalates critical security risks through the project escalation process

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals validate quality and ensure acceptance criteria are met. They develop test plans, execute tests, report defects, and advocate for quality.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and test cases
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate acceptance criteria before release
- Perform regression testing and smoke tests
- Collaborate on quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria
- Catch defects early and maintain quality standards
- Provide confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports with reproduction steps
- Test coverage and quality metrics
- Release readiness assessments

### Key Interactions
- Works closely with Developers to clarify requirements and validate fixes
- Partners with Product Managers on acceptance criteria
- Collaborates with Business Analysts on requirement validation
- Works with UI/UX Designers to validate user experience
- Supports DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers on staging/production testing

---

## Customer Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Support Liaisons represent end-user needs within the project lifecycle. They channel customer feedback, support issues, and pain points into the product planning and delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and synthesize customer feedback and support tickets
- Identify recurring pain points and usability issues
- Share customer insights with Product Managers and Product Teams
- Validate new features from a support readiness perspective
- Create or update support documentation and FAQs
- Participate in release planning to ensure support preparedness

### Goals
- Ensure customer needs inform product decisions
- Reduce support burden and improve customer satisfaction
- Accelerate support team readiness for new releases

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and pain point reports
- Support readiness reviews during release planning
- Customer-facing documentation and FAQs
- Support ticket trend analysis and insights

### Key Interactions
- Partners with Product Managers on customer feedback and priorities
- Shares customer pain points with UI/UX Designers
- Collaborates with QA/Testing on end-user acceptance
- Advises Project Managers on release timing and communication
- Supports Developers with real-world usage scenarios
